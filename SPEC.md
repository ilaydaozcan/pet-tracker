   # Pet Tracker
   ## Who it's for
   Me, for my two kittens
   ## Core features (3)
   Add and organize medical records, help track check-ups and vaccinations, add multiple cat profiles. When I log a care item, the app calculates the next due date from the care rules.
   ## Care rules (cats only)
   Default schedule based on AAHA/AAFP feline guidelines. Shown in the app as "suggested"; the user can change any due date.
| Care item | First due (age) | Repeat | Until / then |
|---|---|---|---|
| FVRCP vaccine | 8 weeks | Every 3 weeks | Until 16 weeks. Booster at 6 months, then every 3 years |
| Rabies vaccine | 12 weeks | Booster 1 year later | Then every 3 years |
| FeLV vaccine | 8 weeks | 2nd dose 3 weeks later | Booster 1 year after 2nd dose, then every 2 years |
| Deworming | 3 weeks | Every 2 weeks | Until 9 weeks, then monthly until 6 months |
| Wellness check-up | 1 year | Every year | Ongoing |
   ## Screens
   Welcome page (with animation if time allows) 
   Home: shows empty state with an add cat profile button, filled state with my cat profiles that shows a way to view their medical records, edit their profile details, and shows what's due next 
   Add pet: add a picture, name, breed, and date of birth
   Pet records: type (a care item from the table, or "Other"), date, and notes
   ## Not in version one
   Photo upload of records, breed rules, reminders, vet booking, and resources
